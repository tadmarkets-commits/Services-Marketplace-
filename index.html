Good to know

Questions, answered.

When does Tidely launch?

We're launching in Waterloo Region soon, starting in Kitchener–Waterloo neighbourhoods. Join the waitlist and we'll email you the moment booking opens in your area.

How is the price decided?

You pick a standard-clean tier based on your bedrooms and bathrooms, then add any extras you want. The full price — HST included — is shown before you book. No quotes, no surprises.

Are the cleaners vetted?

Yes. Every cleaner is background-checked, reference-checked, and insurance-verified before their first job — and reviewed on quality afterward. That's what the "Vetted & Insured" badge stands for.

What if I'm not happy with the clean?

Tell us within 24 hours and we'll make it right — a re-clean or a fair refund, reviewed against the standard checklist. Our satisfaction guarantee backs every booking.

Can I book a recurring clean?

Absolutely. After your first clean you can set up weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly service at a recurring discount — same trusted cleaner whenever possible.
